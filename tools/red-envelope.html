FAQ

How much money goes in a red envelope?

Any amount without a 4; even amounts for weddings and New Year. 88, 168, 288, 520 and 888 are favourites.

Why avoid odd amounts at weddings?

Pairs symbolise union — ‘good things come in pairs’ — so even amounts are customary.
