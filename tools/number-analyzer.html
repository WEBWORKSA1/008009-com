How the score works

  1. Digits — 8 (+10), 9 (+7), 6 (+6), 2 (+3), 3 (+2), 0/1 (+1), 7 (0), 5 (−1), 4 (−10), scaled for length.
  2. Combinations — 168, 518, 888, 520, 1314 add points; 14, 24, 74, 250 subtract.
  3. Ending — ending on 8, 9 or 6 adds; ending on 4 subtracts.
  4. Patterns — repeats, ABAB, ascending runs and palindromes add memorability.
  5. Normalisation — the total is mapped to a 1–99 score.

FAQ

Is my phone number lucky?

Enter it above. Numbers rich in 8, 9 and 6, without 4, ending on 8 or 9, and containing combinations like 168 or 518 score highest.

What is the luckiest phone number?

Solid runs of 8s (e.g. 8888-8888) are considered the luckiest in Chinese culture; one sold for ¥2.33 million in 2003.

Does the analyzer store my number?

No. It runs entirely in your browser.
