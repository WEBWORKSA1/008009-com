## 1. What "008009" means in Chinese culture

Chinese number symbolism runs on **homophones**: Mandarin (and Cantonese) have few syllables, so digits borrow meaning from words they sound like.

| Part | Character | Sounds like / symbol | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | 零零 líng | 圆满 wholeness; "00" is mainland China's international call prefix (0086 = China) | Completeness, a gateway to the world |
| 8 | 八 bā | 发 fā — "prosper, get rich" (发财) | Wealth — the luckiest digit in Chinese culture |
| 00 | 零零 | symmetry | Balance |
| 9 | 九 jiǔ | 久 jiǔ — "long-lasting"; the emperor's number | Longevity, eternity |

**Combined reading:** 8 → 9 = **发久, "prosperity that lasts"**, framed by zeros for wholeness, in a symmetrical `008 · 009` form. The number contains **no 4** (四 sì ≈ 死 sǐ, "death"), so nothing in it is traditionally unlucky. On our transparent scoring engine it lands in the top ("imperial") tier at 91/100.

**Why numeric domains matter for Chinese users:** Chinese characters can't easily be typed into domain names. Numbers are a common language across Mandarin, Cantonese and other dialects, which is why top Chinese brands use numeric domains such as 163.com and 360.cn. Supply is fixed (1,000 NNN.com; 10,000 NNNN.com; 1,000,000 NNNNNN.com).

**People:** lucky numbers shape how people choose wedding dates (8/8, 5/20 = "I love you"), red-envelope amounts (88, 168, 888; never 4), which floors buildings number (4, 14 and 24 are skipped), and the phone numbers, prices and store-opening dates businesses pick. The audience is global: Greater China plus a diaspora of tens of millions.

**Decision:** **008009.com — The Global Lucky Numbers Hub.** Free tools bring in the traffic. The **Lucky Number Exchange** turns it into leads for valuations, pattern alerts and private buyer/seller matching of lucky **phone numbers, licence plates and numeric domains**. It's backed by consultations, sponsorships, contests and donations.

The high-intent pages (e.g. `/numbers/888`) feed a marketplace where a single brokered deal can be worth more than years of AdSense. And because numeric domains are one of the three asset classes, the domain portfolio becomes supply for the Exchange.

## 4. Revenue model

1. **AdSense.** On informational pages only, never between a tool's input and its result. Slots are clearly labelled. Until an AdSense ID is set, house ads fill the slots.
2. **Exchange lead-gen.** Free valuation, pattern alerts, then a success-fee commission. This is the highest-value stream.
3. **Consultations.** Business number/name audit (from $188), date selection (from $88), personal report (from $38), feng shui referrals.
4. **Sponsorships.** Lucky Number of the Day ($88/wk), Tool sponsor ($388/mo), Category exclusive ($888/mo), Contest sponsor (custom).
5. **YouTube.** A library of 12 verified videos now. Add your own channel through `config.js` for its own ad revenue.
6. **Affiliates.** Carriers, registrars, plate dealers, feng shui retailers, language schools, travel.
7. **Donations & memberships.** Stripe, PayPal, Ko-fi, BMC and GitHub Sponsors links are set in config. A pledge form is the fallback, and the funds are tied to operations, marketing, hiring and prizes.
